TikTok

TikTok integration

An authorized TikTok user can connect their TikTok account to Merist using TikTok’s official authorization / OAuth flow. Merist then uses only the scopes TikTok and that user approve.

Merist does not post to arbitrary TikTok accounts. Publishing and upload actions only apply to accounts that have explicitly authorized the Merist app. Merist does not claim ownership of a user’s TikTok account or content merely because the account is connected.

How connection works

  1. The operator starts a TikTok connect flow inside Merist.
  2. TikTok presents its official authorization screen with the requested scopes.
  3. The TikTok account owner reviews and approves (or denies) those permissions.
  4. After approval, Merist can use the granted capabilities for that connected account only.
  5. The owner can revoke Merist in TikTok’s account settings at any time; the operator can also disconnect inside Merist.

Requested TikTok capabilities

user.info.basic
Used to identify the authorized TikTok profile and display basic account information.
user.info.stats
Used to retrieve account-level performance / statistical data available through TikTok.
video.list
Used to retrieve the authorized account’s public TikTok video information for content history and performance workflows.
video.upload
Used to upload authorized Merist-generated content to TikTok as a draft for further creator review, editing, or posting where applicable.
video.publish
Used to directly publish authorized content to the connected TikTok profile after the user has granted the required TikTok permissions.

Analytics / performance workflow

Where TikTok has authorized the relevant read/analytics capabilities and the user has connected their account, Merist can organize permitted profile, stats, and video-list information so operators can review how published content performed and decide what to create next.

Currently available where authorized Analytics and read capabilities depend on TikTok’s grant and the user’s approved scopes. Unavailable fields stay blank.

Publishing workflow

Merist’s content loop prepares media and, when posting scopes are approved and granted, can:

  • Upload content to the connected TikTok account as a draft (video.upload), or
  • Directly publish to that connected profile (video.publish).

Subject to TikTok authorization Direct Post and Upload are integration capabilities Merist is adding. Live use of video.publish and video.upload depends on TikTok’s authorization of those scopes and the user’s consent. This page does not claim those posting scopes are already live if TikTok review is still pending.

Security boundaries

  • Authorization happens on TikTok’s own pages.
  • Merist does not collect TikTok passwords.
  • Access tokens / refresh tokens are handled securely on Merist servers and are not publicly exposed.
  • Actions stay within granted scopes on the connected account only.
