Merist Content Intelligence

Authorized analytics and content publishing for connected social accounts.

Merist is a media brand that helps operators choose and use AI tools with evidence, not hype. Public content is published as @meristhq on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and X.

Merist Content Intelligence is the product behind that loop. It helps operators identify content opportunities, prepare media, connect social accounts they are authorized to use, publish or upload through official platform authorization where scopes allow, measure performance, and use that evidence to improve the next piece of content.

Account connections use each platform’s official authorization (OAuth). Merist does not collect platform passwords. Merist only acts on accounts the user has explicitly authorized, and only within the permissions that user and the platform grant. Merist does not post to arbitrary accounts.

Core capabilities

Connect authorized accounts

The account owner signs in with the platform and grants the scopes Merist needs for analytics and, when approved, publishing or upload.

Create and prepare content

Operators move from opportunity research to scripted, media-ready content intended for supported platforms.

Authorized publish / upload

Where platform approval and user consent allow, Merist can upload drafts or publish to the connected profile — never to accounts that did not authorize the app.

Measure and learn

Permitted performance data is organized so operators can compare posts and improve the next cycle. Missing metrics stay missing.

TikTok status (honest)

Analytics Where TikTok has authorized the app and the user has connected their account, Merist can use permitted profile, stats, and video-list data for performance workflows.

Content Posting Merist is adding TikTok Direct Post (video.publish) and upload (video.upload) as an integration capability. Live use of those publishing scopes depends on TikTok’s authorization and the user’s granted permissions. This site does not claim those posting scopes are already approved if review is still pending.
