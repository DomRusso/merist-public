Merist Content Intelligence

Learn from published content with authorized, read-only analytics.

Merist is a media brand that helps operators choose and use AI tools with evidence, not hype. Public content is published as @meristhq on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and X.

Merist Content Intelligence is the product behind that learning loop. It helps operators connect social accounts they are authorized to use, collect the performance data those platforms permit, organize that information, compare content, and make better decisions about what to create next.

Connected platforms are authorized by the account owner through each platform’s official sign-in. Merist does not collect platform passwords and does not post, upload, or delete content.

Connect authorized accounts

The account owner signs in with the platform and grants analytics access.

Collect permitted analytics

Merist reads identity and performance metrics the platform actually returns.

Organize and compare

Results are grouped by content so operators can see what happened after publish.

Support better decisions

Evidence from prior posts informs later creative choices. Missing metrics stay missing.
