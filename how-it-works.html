How it works

From opportunity to authorized publish and measurement

Merist Content Intelligence does not sign in with your password and does not scrape a private session. The account owner authorizes access through each platform’s official OAuth (or equivalent) flow. Merist then performs only the actions covered by the granted permissions.

End-to-end workflow

  1. 1
    Research a content opportunity

    Identify a topic, angle, and audience worth covering on a supported platform format.

  2. 2
    Create the content

    Produce the script, claim, and creative direction for the piece.

  3. 3
    Prepare media / video

    Assemble the media package (for example a short-form video) ready for the destination platform.

  4. 4
    Connect an authorized platform account

    The operator selects a supported platform and is sent to that platform’s official authorization page. Permissions are reviewed there — not on a Merist password form.

  5. 5
    Authorized platform publishing or upload

    When the platform has approved the relevant app scopes and the user has granted them, Merist can upload content as a draft for further creator review or publish directly to the connected profile. Publishing only occurs for accounts that explicitly authorized the app.

  6. 6
    Performance measurement

    Merist retrieves identity and performance fields the platform returns for the authorized account and its content. Fields the platform does not return remain unavailable.

  7. 7
    Future content optimization

    Organized results inform the next research and creative cycle. Evidence replaces guesswork where the platform provided data.

User control

  • Users explicitly authorize each supported social account.
  • Access is limited to the permissions/scopes granted by the user and the platform.
  • Users can disconnect a platform in Merist and can revoke the app in the platform’s own settings.
  • Merist only performs supported actions within those granted permissions.
  • Credentials and tokens are handled on Merist servers and are not exposed on this public site.

For TikTok specifically — including requested scopes such as user.info.basic, user.info.stats, video.list, video.upload, and video.publish — see TikTok integration.
