How it works

Official authorization, then read-only measurement

Merist Content Intelligence does not sign in as you and does not scrape a password-protected session. The account owner authorizes access through the platform.

1. Choose a connected platform

An operator selects a platform Merist supports, such as YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram. Only accounts the operator is authorized to connect should be used.

2. Sign in with the platform

Merist sends the operator to that platform’s official authorization page. The operator reviews the requested permissions there, not on a Merist password form.

3. Grant analytics access

The account owner approves read-only permissions needed to identify the account and retrieve permitted content analytics. Publishing permission is not requested.

4. Collect permitted metrics

After authorization, Merist retrieves the identity and performance fields the platform returns for the authorized account and for content that account owns. Fields the platform does not return remain unavailable.

5. Organize, compare, and learn

Those results are stored as a performance record. Operators use them to compare published content and to inform later creative decisions.

6. Disconnect or revoke at any time

The operator can disconnect the account in Merist. The owner can also revoke the app in the platform’s own account settings. Either action stops further retrieval.
