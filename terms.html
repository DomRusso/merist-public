Terms of Service

Last updated: 14 August 2026

These terms cover Merist Content Intelligence (“the service”), operated by Merist. The service is currently used by the Merist operator to retrieve official, read-only analytics for Merist’s own published content.

What the service does

After you authorize a platform account through that platform’s official login, the service may read account identity and content performance metrics that the platform exposes to authorized apps.

What the service does not do

  • It does not publish, upload, edit, or delete content.
  • It does not ask for or store your platform password.
  • It does not sell access to your account.

Authorization

You may revoke access at any time in the platform’s app-permissions settings and by disconnecting the account in Merist. Sandbox and development use is limited to accounts the operator is authorized to connect.
