Terms of Service

Last updated: 16 August 2026

These terms cover Merist Content Intelligence (“the service”), operated by Merist. The service retrieves official, read-only analytics for social accounts after the account owner authorizes access.

The service

After you authorize a platform account through that platform’s official login, the service may read account identity and content performance metrics that the platform exposes to authorized apps. The service may organize those results so operators can compare published content and make later creative decisions.

Authorization

Connected platforms are authorized by the account owner. You should only connect accounts you are allowed to authorize. You may revoke access at any time in the platform’s app-permissions settings and by disconnecting the account in Merist.

What the service does not do

  • It does not publish, upload, edit, or delete content.
  • It does not request publishing permission.
  • It does not ask for or store your platform password.
  • It does not sell access to your account.
  • It does not request X Direct Message access.
  • Current X authorization requests identity and read scopes only. Publishing on X stays gated in the product and does not happen from these pages.

Accuracy of metrics

The service reports values returned by the authorized platform. If a platform does not provide a metric, the service leaves it unavailable rather than inventing a number.

Acceptable use

Do not use the service to access accounts you do not control or are not authorized to connect. Do not attempt to use the service to publish or manage content on a platform.

Changes

Merist may update these terms. The date at the top of this page is the latest revision.
