Security

Security and data handling

Merist Content Intelligence is designed so that platform access is granted by the account owner, limited to analytics, and revocable.

Official authorization

Account connections use each platform’s official OAuth or equivalent authorization. Merist does not ask for, collect, or store platform passwords.

Read-only analytics

The product retrieves account identity and content performance metrics that the authorized platform returns. It does not request permission to publish, upload, edit, or delete content.

Credentials and tokens

When a platform issues access and refresh tokens, those tokens are stored on Merist’s servers and encrypted at rest. They are not displayed on these public pages or in ordinary product screens.

Data obtained from platforms

Typical permitted fields include account identifiers, display names, and performance metrics such as views, likes, comments, and shares when the platform provides them. Metrics a platform does not return stay missing. They are not filled with zero.

Disconnection and revocation

An operator can disconnect a platform inside Merist, which removes the stored tokens for that connection. The account owner can also revoke the app in TikTok, Google, or Meta account settings. After revocation, Merist cannot retrieve new analytics for that account.

Use of the data

Data is used to measure Merist content and to inform later creative decisions. It is not sold. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for the governing statements.
