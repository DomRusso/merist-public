Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Merist Content Intelligence retrieves official analytics for Merist-owned social accounts after operator authorization. This policy describes what that service stores.

Data we collect

  • Account identifiers the platform returns after official OAuth (for example account ID and display name).
  • Content identifiers and performance metrics the platform returns (for example views, likes, comments, shares when available).
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, stored only on Merist’s servers, encrypted, and never shown on these pages or in the product screens.

Data we do not collect

  • Platform passwords.
  • Payment card numbers.
  • Metrics a platform does not actually return. Missing values stay missing; they are not filled with zero.

Use

Data is used to measure Merist content and to improve later creative decisions. It is not sold. Publishing permissions are not requested.

Retention and revocation

Tokens can be deleted by disconnecting the platform connection. You can also revoke the app in TikTok, Google, or Meta account settings.
