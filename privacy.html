Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Merist Content Intelligence retrieves official analytics for social accounts after the account owner authorizes access. This policy describes what that service stores and how it is used.

Data we collect

  • Account identifiers the platform returns after official OAuth, such as an account ID, display name, and public handle when provided.
  • Content identifiers and performance metrics the platform returns, such as views, likes, comments, and shares when available.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by the platform. These are stored only on Merist’s servers, encrypted, and are not shown on these pages or in ordinary product screens.

Data we do not collect

  • Platform passwords.
  • Payment card numbers.
  • Metrics a platform does not actually return. Missing values stay missing; they are not filled with zero.

How we obtain data

Data comes from authorized third-party platforms (including YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and X when connected) after the account owner completes that platform’s official authorization. Merist does not scrape passwords or use unofficial logins.

Use

Data is used to measure published content, organize performance information, compare content, and support later creative decisions. It is not sold. X Direct Messages are not requested. Publishing on X stays gated until a separate operator authorization; this website does not post.

Retention and revocation

Tokens can be deleted by disconnecting the platform connection in Merist. You can also revoke the app in TikTok, Google, Meta, or X account settings. After disconnect or revocation, Merist cannot retrieve new analytics for that account.
