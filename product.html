Product

What Merist Content Intelligence does

The product is for operators who already publish content and need a reliable record of how that content performed.

Features

  • Connect authorized social accounts. The account owner uses the platform’s official authorization flow. Merist only proceeds after that owner grants access.
  • Collect permitted content analytics. After authorization, Merist retrieves account identity and content metrics that the platform exposes to authorized apps.
  • Organize performance information. Metrics are stored against specific published items so operators can review them later.
  • Compare content performance. Operators can see which published items attracted views, likes, comments, shares, or other metrics the platform actually provided.
  • Learn from previously published content. The point of the product is evidence after publish, not guessing before it.
  • Support content decision-making. Organized results help operators decide what to make next. Merist does not autonomously post those decisions.

What the product does not do

  • It does not publish, upload, edit, or delete videos or comments.
  • It does not ask for posting permission.
  • It does not invent metrics. If a platform does not return a value, Merist leaves it blank.
  • It does not collect platform passwords.

Platform coverage and current limits are described on Supported platforms. Data handling is described on Security and in the Privacy Policy.
