Platforms

Supported platforms

Merist Content Intelligence can connect the following platforms through each platform’s official authorization. A platform is only used after the account owner grants access.

YouTube

Merist can connect a YouTube channel the operator is authorized to use. After official Google authorization, Merist may read channel identity and permitted video analytics. Publishing and upload permissions are not requested.

TikTok

Merist can connect a TikTok account the operator is authorized to use. After official TikTok authorization, Merist may read basic profile information, profile statistics the platform exposes, and public video list/performance fields needed to measure published content.

TikTok does not grant Merist permission to post, upload, or manage videos. Some studio-only metrics may be unavailable; unavailable values are left blank.

Instagram

Merist can connect an Instagram professional account through official Meta authorization when the operator enables that connection. After authorization, Merist may read permitted media insights. Publishing permission is not requested.

Shared boundary

  • Authorization always happens on the platform’s own sign-in page.
  • Merist requests analytics access, not posting access.
  • The operator may disconnect a platform in Merist at any time.
  • The account owner may revoke the app in that platform’s settings.
